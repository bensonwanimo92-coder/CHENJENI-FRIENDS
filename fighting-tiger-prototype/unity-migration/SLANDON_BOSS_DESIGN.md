# Big Boss Slandon

- **Presence:** intimidating but human-sized and believable; no exaggerated anatomy.
- **Fight:** three readable phases, not a damage sponge.
  1. Pressure: measured punches, body shots and grabs with clear wind-ups.
  2. Adaptation: feints, guard, counter-punches; punish predictable attacks.
  3. Desperation: faster combination and one arena pressure move; create recovery openings.
- **Fairness:** clear anticipation, dodgeable attacks, stamina windows, vulnerability after committed moves.
- **Story outcome:** defeat Slandon, rescue Odessa and end on a cinematic reunion.
- **Implementation status:** design specification only; not yet implemented/tested in Unity.
