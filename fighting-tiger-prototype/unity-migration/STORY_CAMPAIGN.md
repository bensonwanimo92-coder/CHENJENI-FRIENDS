# Street Legend — Story Campaign

## Opening: The Hotel
Wanimoo takes his daughter Odessa to a hotel for a quiet outing. The evening turns violent when a gang storms the place, grabs Odessa, and escapes. Wanimoo fights through the attackers and discovers clues leading to the gang's hideouts. The campaign follows his pursuit and rescue attempt.

## Four levels

### Level 1 — Hotel Escape
**Setting:** hotel lobby, restaurant, service corridor and parking area.
**Objective:** survive the ambush, defeat the gang's first wave, find the kidnappers' trail and escape the hotel.
**Enemy mix:** basic thugs, a quick attacker, a guard blocking the exit.
**Mini-boss:** hotel enforcer.
**Story beat:** Wanimoo finds a gang symbol and a clue pointing to their street territory.

### Level 2 — Backstreet Pursuit
**Setting:** crowded alleyways, small shops, loading yards and rooftops/raised walkways.
**Objective:** track the van and fight through gang patrols.
**Enemy mix:** street fighters, aggressive rushers, guarded bruisers; enemies should telegraph their attacks.
**Mini-boss:** gang tracker.
**Story beat:** the tracker reveals the gang is working for a larger operation.

### Level 3 — Gang Hideout
**Setting:** warehouse compound, underground garage and gang hideout interior.
**Objective:** break into the compound, defeat the guards and uncover where Odessa is being held.
**Enemy mix:** coordinated pairs, defensive fighters, heavy enforcers.
**Mini-boss:** hideout lieutenant.
**Story beat:** Wanimoo discovers the kidnapping was ordered by Slandon.

### Level 4 — Slandon's Stronghold
**Setting:** fortified industrial headquarters with a dramatic main hall and boss arena.
**Objective:** defeat Slandon's guards, reach the arena and face Big Boss Slandon.
**Boss:** Slandon — multiple phases, clear attack telegraphs, stagger windows, counter opportunities and a final cinematic defeat.
**Story beat:** rescue Odessa. End with an emotional reunion and campaign completion.

## Combat design
- Keep character anatomy, outfits and motion grounded and believable; avoid cartoonish oversized muscles.
- Use enemy archetypes with distinguishable attacks, readable anticipation and recovery.
- Every hard hit must trigger an animation response and interruption/stagger where appropriate, rather than health loss alone.
- Boss phases should teach patterns and reward dodging, blocking and timing.
- Story transitions should use short cinematic sequences that can be skipped.
- The four-level structure is a campaign design target; Unity implementation and assets are not yet complete.
