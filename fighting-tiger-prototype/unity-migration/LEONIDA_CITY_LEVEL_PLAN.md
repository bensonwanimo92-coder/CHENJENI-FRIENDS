# Leonida City — Street Legend Environment Plan

Leonida City is the urban reference for expanding Street Legend's campaign environments. Use an original, grounded city layout (do not copy GTA/Rockstar assets, maps, logos, characters, or exact locations).

## Design goals
- Dense, believable city blocks with distinct streets and landmarks.
- Keep the core rescue story linear across four combat chapters, while connecting them through a larger city backdrop.
- Human-scale buildings, alleyways, service roads, storefronts, parking lots, loading areas and industrial spaces.
- Visual storytelling: signage, lighting, weather, crowd silhouettes, parked vehicles and debris.
- Prioritize Android performance: modular environment pieces, reusable materials, limited realtime lights, LOD/culling and simple collision meshes.

## Campaign route through Leonida City
1. **Hotel District — Hotel Escape:** hotel lobby/restaurant, service corridor, loading bay and parking. Wanimoo and Odessa are visiting when Odessa is kidnapped. The initial escape and first confrontation occur here.
2. **Downtown Backstreets — Backstreet Pursuit:** storefront alley, bus stop, market lane, underpass and delivery yard. Track the kidnappers and confront their tracker.
3. **Industrial Fringe — Gang Hideout:** warehouse floor, office mezzanine, garage, fenced yard and gang checkpoint. Fight coordinated opponents and learn that Slandon is behind the kidnapping.
4. **Stronghold — Slandon's Compound:** factory/industrial headquarters, courtyard, interior corridors and boss arena. Defeat Slandon, rescue Odessa and finish with a reunion cutscene.

## City composition / modular kit
- Street segments: straight, corner, T-junction, crossroad and narrow alley.
- Props: bins, bollards, crates, barriers, streetlights, signs, benches and vending machines.
- Building modules: shopfront, hotel facade, warehouse, office block, apartments and garages.
- Combat spaces should avoid clutter that traps player/enemies; props can add visual cover but are not obstacles unless deliberately collidable.
- District transitions may use short cinematic camera pans, loading gates or fades rather than a seamless open world in the initial Android build.

## Visual direction
Photorealistic aspirations with restrained, realistic proportions. Use original textures, procedural materials and legally usable assets only. Avoid oversized/cartoon fighters and excessive visual effects. City palette: warm hotel lighting, cool downtown night lighting, sodium-lit alleys, industrial neutral tones and strong boss-arena contrast.

## Scope note
This document is a level-planning reference, not a completed or tested Unity map. Actual scene construction, assets, lighting, collision, gameplay and device performance remain pending.
