# Street Legend combat feel

The fight should be demanding, athletic and skill-driven—not button-mashing.

## Move vocabulary to animate
- Straight punches, jab-cross, hooks, body shots, uppercut.
- Front kick, roundhouse, side kick, low kick, knee strike.
- Sweeps and controlled takedowns; grounded recovery.
- Guard, parry, evade, step-back and counter.
- Combo chains with timing windows and stamina costs.

## Design principles
- Each attack needs wind-up, active contact frames and recovery.
- Use hit reaction clips and interruption rules: impacts must visibly affect the target.
- Heavy attacks require commitment and stamina; missed attacks leave an opening.
- Enemy archetypes vary: fast striker, kicker, defensive counter-fighter and heavy bruiser.
- Opponents defend, flank, attack in limited coordinated groups and recover after combos.
- Boss Slandon should have distinct phases and punish repeated tactics without unfair unavoidable damage.
- Difficulty escalates across four levels, with checkpoints and readable telegraphs.
- Animation, sound, timing and hit detection must be synchronized.
- Actual animation assets and editor testing remain required; this document and script alone do not produce finished moves.
